How the cat works

  • Drag the cursor. Press and drag the purple arrow around the page. The cat's eyes follow it wherever it goes.
  • Hover over the cat. When the arrow is on the cat, he gets happy.
  • Hover over his head. A little "Hold" appears, a hint that he'd like you to stay there.
  • Hold it there. Keep the arrow pressed on the cat for a moment and hearts float up. Good petting!
  • Don't touch his tummy! Poke the stomach and the cat gets angry, and he stays angry until he's fed.
  • Feed him the fish. When he's angry a fish appears. Drag it onto the cat and he'll eat it and be happy again.
